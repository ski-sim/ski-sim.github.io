\section{Research Interest}
I am broadly interested in leveraging Machine Learning for Combinatorial Optimization (ML4CO)
to solve complex, real-world problems, with a particular focus on integrating ML with
Mixed-Integer Linear Programming (MILP). Currently, my research centers on exploring and
developing discrete diffusion models to tackle these optimization challenges.
